使用帮助

  • 文档扫描支持自动或手动拍摄。拍完后可裁切、调整页序，并分享 PDF 或 JPG。
  • 扫描证件时，选择身份证、护照或其他证件模板。拍完可预览 A4 排版。
  • 在「提取文字」选择识别语言。导出前可以校对结果。翻译和表格识别取决于设备的系统版本。
  • Plus 通过 Apple 购买。换机后可在应用内恢复购买，续订可在 Apple 账户的订阅设置中管理。

反馈问题。请附上轻扫版本、iOS 版本和重现步骤。请勿上传证件、扫描件或其他个人资料。

Support

  • Scan a document automatically or with the shutter button. Crop, reorder, and share your scans as PDF or JPG.
  • Use ID Scan to prepare an A4 copy of a card, passport, or other document. Preview the page before export.
  • Choose a recognition language under Extract Text and proofread the result before exporting. Translation and table recognition depend on your iOS version.
  • Plus purchases are handled by Apple. Restore purchases in QingScan and manage renewals in your Apple Account settings.

Report an issue. Include the app version, iOS version, and steps to reproduce. Please omit scans, IDs, and other personal information.
