轻扫隐私政策

轻扫无需注册。应用开发者不通过轻扫收集个人数据，应用不包含广告跟踪或开发者分析代码。

相机仅用于你发起的扫描或拍照。你也可以主动选择照片和文件。扫描、裁切、文字识别和导出在设备上完成。拍照翻译使用 Apple 的系统翻译功能，首次使用可能下载语言包。

扫描档案存储在设备上，可在应用内删除。你选择分享时，系统会把文件交给你选择的应用或服务。已经导出的副本可在保存它们的位置删除。

订阅和付款由 Apple 处理。轻扫使用 StoreKit 确认当前购买权益。开发者不会通过轻扫获取你的付款凭证或支付账号。

QingScan Privacy Policy

QingScan does not require an account. The developer does not collect personal data through QingScan. The app contains no advertising trackers or developer analytics.

The camera is used for scans and photos you initiate. You can also choose specific photos and files to import. Scanning, cropping, text recognition, and export run on your device. Photo translation uses Apple's system translation, which may download language packs when first used.

Your scan archive is stored on your device and can be deleted within QingScan. If you share a file, iOS passes it to the destination you select. You can delete exported copies wherever you saved them.

Apple handles subscription payments. QingScan uses StoreKit to confirm your current entitlements. The developer does not obtain your payment credentials or payment account through the app.
